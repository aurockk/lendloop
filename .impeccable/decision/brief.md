# Surface brief: LendLoop (landing + board screens)

Scope: replacement visual world for the whole app. Landing `/` is Persuade; `/tools`, `/listings`, `/requests`, `/incoming`, `/admin` are Operate and inherit the world with scanability first.
Audience/job: neighbours finding, lending and returning tools; Fluence reviewers judging craft in seconds. Constraints: all functionality, English copy, LendLoop name and tagline kept; must not read as generic SaaS, childish, hard to use, or slow.
Memorable moment: an available tool lifts off the board and reveals its painted outline; a tool on loan is only its outline.

## Direction contract

THESIS: LendLoop is the neighbourhood's shadow board — every tool has a painted place, and an empty outline means it is out on loan. Refuses the marketplace grid of photo cards on white.

OWN-WORLD: perforated hardboard tan ground (#B4895A) with a regular hole grid; tools as ink-black silhouettes (#141414) hanging over their painted outlines; safety-yellow (#F5C518) paint for the one primary action and active states; white paint (#F7F4EE) for panels where reading or forms happen; stencil display (Big Shoulders Stencil) painted on the board, Archivo for UI text. Square-ish painted corners, hand-painted label strips, no soft cards.

STORY: visitor sees the tagline stencilled across the board, real tools hanging in their outlines, understands outline = out on loan from the board itself, signs in to lend or borrow; inside, the same board is the catalogue and each loan is a strip with a date rail.

FIRST VIEWPORT: tagline stencilled huge across the top-left of the board (two lines, black paint); right/below it the live shadow board of public tools (silhouette per category, painted name label); a yellow painted plate "Sign in to lend or borrow" under the tagline; a three-slot legend (hanging / outline only / tagged by you) labelled as an example.

FORM: The Shadow Board, my rank 1 of 7, chosen as IMPECCABLE'S PICK; seed key b0542a65.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
